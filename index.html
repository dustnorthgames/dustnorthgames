Welcome to Dominion City

The city shines brightest just before everything falls apart.

Concrete Reign is a cinematic open-world action game set across palm-lined coastlines, dense downtown streets and districts that transform after dark. Drive, explore, take on story missions and shape your place in a city that never truly sleeps.
